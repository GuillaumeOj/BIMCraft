# Introduction

BIMCraft is a BIM authoring tool for buildings and civil works. You build one shared model of your project, and BIMCraft produces 3D views, plans, sections, sheets and calculation reports from it.

This guide is written for the people who use BIMCraft every day: architects, structural and MEP engineers, drafters and BIM managers. It is organised by task: find what you want to do in the menu and follow the steps.

> BIMCraft is in an early phase and is not yet ready for production use. Pages are added here as features become available.

Calculation results must always be checked by a qualified engineer before being used for design or construction.
