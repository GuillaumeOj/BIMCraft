# Introduction

Fabrilo is a BIM authoring tool for buildings and civil works. You build one shared model of your project, and Fabrilo produces 3D views, plans, sections, sheets and calculation reports from it.

> **New name.** Fabrilo was called BIMCraft until November 2026. It is the same product: older links, screenshots and videos may still show the old name.

This guide is written for the people who use Fabrilo every day: architects, structural and MEP engineers, drafters and BIM managers. It is organised by task: find what you want to do in the menu and follow the steps.

> Fabrilo is in an early phase and is not yet ready for production use. Pages are added here as features become available.

Calculation results must always be checked by a qualified engineer before being used for design or construction.
